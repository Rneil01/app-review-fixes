iPad screenshots: required sizes and avoiding 2.3 rejections

If your app runs on iPad, App Store Connect needs at least one 13-inch iPad screenshot. And they have to actually show iPad.

Sizes Apple accepts (from Apple's screenshot specifications)

  • iPad 13-inch, required if your app runs on iPad: 2064 x 2752 or 2048 x 2732 pixels (portrait), or the landscape versions 2752 x 2064 and 2732 x 2048.
  • iPhone, required: at least one set for the "iPhone with Dynamic Island" sizes, for example 1179 x 2556 or 1206 x 2622 portrait. Larger sets like 1320 x 2868 are optional.
  • 1 to 10 screenshots per size, in .png or .jpg/.jpeg, with no transparency.

Check Apple's current screenshot specifications before uploading, because sizes change with new devices.

Does my Expo app "run on iPad"?

If ios.supportsTablet is true in your Expo config, your app is a native iPad app and needs iPad screenshots. That's how mine ended up needing them. Either way, test it on an iPad, because reviewers often use one.

Avoiding guideline 2.3 (accurate metadata)

  • Your iPad screenshots must show the app running on iPad. A stretched iPhone screenshot isn't enough.
  • Show real screens from the current build, not mockups of features that don't exist yet.
  • Keep text overlays honest: no prices or claims that don't match the app.

Quick way to capture them

Run the app in the Xcode Simulator on an "iPad Pro 13-inch" device and press Cmd+S to save a screenshot. Check the pixel size before uploading.
