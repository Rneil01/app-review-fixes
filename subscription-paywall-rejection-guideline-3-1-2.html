Subscription paywall rejected: what 3.1.2 requires

Most paywall rejections come from a missing link or a price that isn't clear. Check these before you submit.

On the paywall (in the app)

  • The subscription title, the length (for example, monthly or yearly), and the price per period.
  • The billed amount is the most prominent price on screen. A "per week" price can't be bigger than the real yearly charge.
  • Working links to your Terms of Use (EULA) and Privacy Policy.
  • A visible Restore Purchases button. Guideline 3.1.1 rejections often cite a missing restore option.

In App Store Connect

  • Privacy Policy URL filled in.
  • If you use Apple's standard EULA, add a Terms of Use link to your App Description. Apps without one get a template rejection.
  • Each subscription has an App Review screenshot of the paywall.

Value

Guideline 3.1.2(a) says a subscription "must provide ongoing value to the customer." In your review notes, say briefly what subscribers get over time.
