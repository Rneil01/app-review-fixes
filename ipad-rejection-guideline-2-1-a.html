Rejected on iPad (2.1(a)) even though you built an iPhone app

Reviewers often test on iPad. If your app gets stuck, crashes or can't complete a purchase on iPad, it's rejected.

Guideline 2.1(a) covers apps that "crash or exhibit obvious technical problems." Guideline 2.4.1 says iPhone apps should run on iPad whenever possible. Apple has told developers that "all apps designed for use on iPhone must still be formatted correctly and behave properly when running on iPad."

This happened to me. One of my rejections came from a reviewer on an iPad Air: the app got stuck on the welcome screen, and the same review flagged that the in-app purchase wasn't submitted.

What to check before resubmitting

  1. Run the app on an iPad simulator or device with the latest iPadOS, from a fresh install. Go through onboarding, login and the paywall.
  2. Check your device setting. In Expo, ios.supportsTablet in your app config controls whether the app runs as a native iPad app. Whichever you choose, test the iPad experience. If you support iPad, App Store Connect will also ask for iPad screenshots.
  3. Test a sandbox purchase on iPad. Developers have reported purchase failures that only happen on iPad, for example a Flutter StoreKit 2 issue on iPadOS 27 (flutter#194373).
  4. Look for layouts that assume a phone screen: fixed heights, buttons pushed off screen, and keyboard covering inputs.
  5. In the Resolution Center, state the device and OS version you tested on and what you fixed.
